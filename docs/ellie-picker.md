Ellie's native picker uses the existing usdRig biped Body and Face artwork. Selection targets are the editable `RigExecControl` channel prims of `RIG-Ellie`; the META armature and evaluated joints are excluded. The packaged template has no dependency on the sibling examples directory.

The source inventory has 1,887 main-armature bones. It includes all 755 bones with custom shapes, including hidden FK and detail controls, and 11 additional unlocked bones in explicit Face Main and Clothes Main collections. All 766 have a unique entry on collection pages. Body and Face add 157 semantic selection aliases matched by side and anatomical role. Collection pages are split at 48 buttons so fitting a page keeps its controls readable. Multiple source collection memberships are retained in the inventory even though each control has one library entry.

The 11 additional controls are `MSTR-H-Head_Bottom`, `P-CTR-CheekPuff.L/R`, `P-Button1/2/3`, and `P-Pin1/2/3/4/5`. Prefixes such as STR, ORG, and ROOT are not grounds for omission. Bones without shape or explicit control collection, or without editable channels, are listed with exclusion reasons in `blender:controlInventory` metadata.

There are also 115 numeric source settings, including IK/FK blending, stretch, hinge, parent selection, and character quality/visibility settings. Their source driver behavior is not yet translated to native editable attributes. They are retained as explicitly unavailable scene metadata on three authored settings panels, with the source owner, property, value, bounds, and reason. The stock picker intentionally hides panels with zero live actions, so these settings panels are not shown in the UI. Selection coverage does not imply those behavioral settings are functional. Biped template actions without a corresponding supported Ellie action have no functional command binding, and no original biped control/attribute targets or commands remain. The 290 native mirror button pairings are retained and remapped inside Ellie’s picker.

Run `python3 tests/testPickerInventory.py` for classification regressions. `tests/checkElliePicker.py` verifies a converted stage against an independent source inventory, checks the 766-control denominator, verifies native editable targets and META exclusion, checks collection deduplication, and requires all 115 settings to carry explicit unavailable reasons. The stock usdview interaction test is `tests/testUsdviewPickerCoverage.py`; it prefers Body/Face aliases, clicks a control from every visible panel, verifies Body/Face Alt-click mirror selection, and reports all 115 unsupported settings as hidden metadata.

The final packaged stage `build/ellie-head-picker/Ellie-final.usdz` passed the stock viewer proof: 766 live source control targets, 47 mouse selection checks across 42 visible panels, two Alt-click mirror selections (upper arms and eyes), and three hidden settings panels containing 48, 48, and 19 unsupported settings. The package bytes remained unchanged. Proof artifacts are in `build/ellie-head-picker/picker-ui-final/`. This verifies desktop selection behavior; device behavior requires separate validation.
